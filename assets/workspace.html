    Keyboard shortcuts

    They work from inside a terminal too.

    Alt+1 to Alt+9
    Go to pane
    Alt+\
    Split the pane to the right
    Alt+-
    Split the pane down
    Alt+X
    Close the pane
    Alt+T
    New tab
    Alt+[ and Alt+]
    Previous and next tab
    Alt+↑ and Alt+↓
    Previous and next workspace
